# PAVE — Save Smarter. Grow Together. Build Wealth.

> **PAVE** is a digital wallet designed for smart savings and traditional thrift contributions (**Ajo / Esusu**). Build lasting financial habits and discipline through automated savings, goal tracking, and community-powered wealth creation.

Licensed by the **Central Bank of Nigeria (CBN)** with bank-grade encryption and 24/7 support.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 💳 Digital Wallet
* **Instant Wallet Funding:** Deposit funds smoothly using debit/credit card, bank transfer, or USSD.
* **Direct Bank Withdrawals:** Withdraw funds directly to your verified bank account anytime.

### 2. 🎯 Smart Savings
* **Flexible Savings:** Save with the freedom to withdraw your money whenever you need it.
* **Fixed Savings:** Lock funds for designated periods to earn competitive, higher interest rates (early withdrawals incur a small penalty).
* **Automated Deposits:** Keep your savings consistent with automatic debits directly from your digital wallet.
* **Visual Goal Tracking:** Monitor your savings progress against set financial targets (e.g., Emergency Fund, vacation, or major purchases).

### 3. 👥 Thrift (Ajo / Esusu)
* **Digitized Cultural Savings:** Experience the tradition of community contribution groups with the convenience and transparency of a modern digital app.
* **Custom Intervals:** Join or create contribution cycles running on daily, weekly, or monthly schedules.
* **Automated Wallet Deductions:** Seamless scheduled debits ensure you never miss a contribution round.
* **Contribution Calendar & Accountability:** Track every cycle on your contribution calendar. Built-in penalties for missed contributions protect group integrity and accountability.

### 4. 🛡️ Security & Regulatory Compliance
* **CBN Licensed:** Fully licensed by the Central Bank of Nigeria.
* **Bank-Grade Encryption:** State-of-the-art encryption standards safeguarding all sensitive transactions and balances.
* **Mandatory KYC Verification:** Mandatory identity verification using your Bank Verification Number (BVN) and National Identity Number (NIN) to protect accounts from fraud.
* **Funds Protection:** Client funds are securely held and insured.

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

<details>
<summary><strong>Is my money safe with PAVE?</strong></summary>
<br>
Yes. PAVE uses bank-grade encryption and is licensed by the Central Bank of Nigeria. Your funds are held securely and insured.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What is KYC and why is it required?</strong></summary>
<br>
KYC (Know Your Customer) verification confirms your identity using your BVN and NIN. This protects you from fraud and is required by regulation before you can make transactions.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Can I withdraw my savings anytime?</strong></summary>
<br>
Flexible savings can be withdrawn anytime. Fixed savings have a lock period, and early withdrawal incurs a small penalty.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How does Thrift (Ajo) work?</strong></summary>
<br>
You contribute a fixed amount at regular intervals (daily, weekly, or monthly). After a set period, you receive your total contributions. Missing contributions may incur penalties.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>What payment methods do you accept?</strong></summary>
<br>
You can fund your wallet via debit/credit card, bank transfer, or USSD. Withdrawals are sent directly to your verified bank account.
</details>

## 💻 Getting Started Locally

### Prerequisites
* **Node.js** (v18 or higher recommended)
* **npm** or package manager of choice

### Installation & Run

1. **Clone the repository:**
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/Femi-Benjamin/Pave-fintech-app.git
   cd pave-fintech-app
   ```

2. **Install dependencies:**
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```

3. **Start development server:**
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```

4. **Build for production:**
   ```bash
   npm run build
   ```
